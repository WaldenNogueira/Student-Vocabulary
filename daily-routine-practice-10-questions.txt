LESSON:
Lesson 1 — Daily Routine

QUESTION:
TYPE:
multiple_choice
PROMPT:
I usually ______ at 6:30, but I stay in bed for another ten minutes.
OPTIONS:
- wake up
- get up
- work out
- go to bed
ANSWER:
wake up
EXPLANATION:
“Wake up” means to stop sleeping. “Get up” means to leave the bed.

QUESTION:
TYPE:
multiple_choice
PROMPT:
After I wake up, I usually ______ right away and take a shower.
OPTIONS:
- get up
- commute
- run errands
- eventually
ANSWER:
get up
EXPLANATION:
“Get up” means to leave your bed or stand up.

QUESTION:
TYPE:
multiple_choice
PROMPT:
My daily ______ takes about forty minutes by bus.
OPTIONS:
- commute
- workload
- alarm
- workout
ANSWER:
commute
EXPLANATION:
A “commute” is the regular trip between home and work or school.

QUESTION:
TYPE:
multiple_choice
PROMPT:
I need to ______ after work: go to the bank, buy groceries, and pick up a package.
OPTIONS:
- run errands
- wake up
- commute
- go to bed
ANSWER:
run errands
EXPLANATION:
“Run errands” means to make several short trips to complete practical tasks.

QUESTION:
TYPE:
multiple_choice
PROMPT:
I try to ______ three times a week to stay healthy.
OPTIONS:
- work out
- get up
- go to bed
- commute
ANSWER:
work out
EXPLANATION:
“Work out” means to exercise.

QUESTION:
TYPE:
multiple_choice
PROMPT:
I was exhausted, so I decided to ______ early.
OPTIONS:
- go to bed
- run errands
- wake up
- squeeze in
ANSWER:
go to bed
EXPLANATION:
“Go to bed” means to get into bed because you intend to sleep.

QUESTION:
TYPE:
fill_blank
PROMPT:
My ______ at 6:00 every morning.
OPTIONS:
- alarm goes off
- alarm gets up
- alarm works out
- alarm commutes
ANSWER:
alarm goes off
EXPLANATION:
When an alarm “goes off,” it starts making a sound.

QUESTION:
TYPE:
multiple_choice
PROMPT:
I only had twenty minutes, but I managed to ______ a quick workout.
OPTIONS:
- squeeze in
- go to bed
- run errands
- wake up
ANSWER:
squeeze in
EXPLANATION:
“Squeeze in” means to find time for something in a busy schedule.

QUESTION:
TYPE:
translate_to_english
PROMPT:
Na verdade, eu trabalho de casa às sextas-feiras.
ANSWER:
Actually, I work from home on Fridays.|Actually, I work from home Fridays.
EXPLANATION:
Use “actually” to correct, clarify, or add information. It does not mean “currently.”

QUESTION:
TYPE:
translate_to_english
PROMPT:
No fim, eu consegui terminar tudo.
ANSWER:
Eventually, I managed to finish everything.|I eventually managed to finish everything.
EXPLANATION:
“Eventually” means “finally” or “after some time,” not “possibly.”
